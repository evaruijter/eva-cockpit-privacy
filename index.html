Eva Cockpit - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Eva Cockpit is a personal, non-commercial project intended to help its owner understand WHOOP sleep, recovery and activity data.

Current status

The WHOOP integration is under development. No WHOOP data is currently collected through this integration.

Intended data use

Subject to authorization, the integration is intended to access recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, daily strain and workout information. Data will be used for personal wellbeing insights and planning.

Storage and third parties

Storage, retention and any third-party processors will be documented here before the integration becomes operational. WHOOP data will not be published in this public GitHub repository.

Control and deletion

The account owner may revoke access through WHOOP. A data deletion process will be specified before data collection begins.
